A running collection. No commentary — if a line needs me to explain it, it has not earned its place here.

> Sometimes markets ignore these fundamentals, and all of a sudden they focus on them. When they do, if you
> don't have your house in order, it's too late.

**Mark Carney** — statement and Q&A on the collapsed Canada–U.S. trade negotiations, 22 August 2026.

> The market is a stern enforcer that unmercifully and unfailingly extracts harsh fines for all (trading)
> transgressions.

**Jack D. Schwager** — *The New Market Wizards*, 1992.

> More is learned from one's errors than from one's successes.

**Primo Levi**
